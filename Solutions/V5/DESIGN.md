## Overview and Status

V5 formalizes the full calculus of Nisht & Abel (2024), *Termination Checking of Copatterns by
Size-Change Termination*, together with the semantic soundness proof (Chapters 4–5), the reduction
of lexicographic termination measures in System `F_ω^{cop}` to size-change termination in System
`F_ω^{cop,SCT}` (Section 5.5, Theorem 6), and the complete inference pipeline from System `F_ω^c`
to System `F_ω^{cop,SCT}` (Chapter 6, Theorems 7–15 and Lemmas 3–5).

Every theorem and lemma builds with zero `sorry`, zero `admit`, and only Lean's standard axioms
`[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`:

- **Theorem 1** (`callRel_wf` in `Solutions/V5/Main.lean`, Ramsey discharged in `FinalCore.lean`):
  the semantic call relation induced by a size-change valid invocation graph family is
  well-founded.
- **Theorem 2** (`sizeWf_sound`, `sizeLe_sound2`, `sizeLt_sound2` in `Solutions/V5/Size.lean`,
  `kinding_sound` in `Solutions/V5/KindSound.lean`, `subTy_sound` in `Solutions/V5/SubSound.lean`):
  two-valuation soundness of size well-formedness, `≤`, and `<`; semantic soundness of kinding
  `Ψ; Δ; Δb; m ⊢ F : κ` and polarized subtyping `Ψ; Δ ⊢ A ≤ B : κ`.
- **Theorem 3** (`fundamental` in `Solutions/V5/Fundamental.lean`): fundamental lemma of
  expression and clause typing under guarded block validity.
- **Theorem 4, Lemma 2, Theorem 5** (`theorem_4_core`, `blocks_valid`, `theorem_5_core` in
  `Solutions/V5/Main.lean` and `Solutions/V5/FinalCore.lean`): soundness of mutual blocks,
  ordered block sequences, and complete programs (`SN blocks.flatten main`).
- **Theorem 6** (`theorem_6_proof` in `Solutions/V5/Theorem6.lean`): every mutual block typed in
  System `F_ω^{cop}` with a duplicate-free lexicographic termination measure (`LexBlockTy sig Ξ`)
  is well-typed in System `F_ω^{cop,SCT}` (`BlockTy sig Ξ`).
- **Theorem 7 & Lemma 3** (`theorem_7`, `lemma_3` in `Solutions/V5/InfEncode.lean`): type encoding
  `π; Δ ⊢_SCT^{π*} A ⇒ Ψ; A'` preserves kinding (`KindingC Ψ Δ A' .star` and
  `SizeCtxWf (Ψ₀ ++ Ψ)`), and every size parameter `i ∈ Ψ.dom` generated by signature encoding
  `+; Δ ⊢_SCT^− A ⇒ Ψ; A'` occurs exclusively either negatively as a `μ`-index or positively as a
  `ν`-index in `A'`.
- **Theorems 8 & 9** (`inferPat_sound`, `inferSpine_sound` in `Solutions/V5/InfPat.lean`): pattern
  and copattern-spine inference produce declarative `PatTy` and `SpineTy` derivations.
- **Lemmas 4 & 5** (`lemma_4`, `lemma_5` in `Solutions/V5/InfGraph.lean`): rigid size variables
  introduced by patterns (`InferPat`) and copattern spines (`InferSpine`) have rigid names and

- **Theorems 10, 11 & 12** (`theorem_10`, `theorems_11_and_12` in `Solutions/V5/InfGraph.lean`):

- **Theorem 13** (`inference_sound` in `Solutions/V5/InfSound.lean`): under a coherent,

- **Theorem 14** (`theorem_14` in `Solutions/V5/InfMain.lean`): when Algorithm 1's solver output

## What V5 adds to V4

1. **Full size algebra (Section 4.1.2)**:
   `a ::= ∞ + n | i + n | k + n (bound) | a ∧ b`, with bound normalization `a^↑` mapping
   `∞ + n ↦ ∞ + 1` and preserving variables and meets.
2. **Kinds with variance and subkinding (Section 4.1.1)**:
   `κ ::= * | π κ₁ → κ₂` over the polarity lattice `◦ ≤ +, − ≤ ⊤`, with polarized context
   inversion `π⁻¹ Δ` and subkinding `SubKind` contravariant in domain polarity/kind and covariant
   in codomain kind.
3. **Higher-kinded type operators and impredicative polymorphism (Section 4.2)**:
   `λ(X : ι). F`, `F G` annotated with the argument's simple kind `ι`, `∀(X : κ). B`, and
   `∃(X : κ). B` at arbitrary kinds `κ`.
4. **Mixed-variance functors for `μ^a S` and `ν^a R` (Section 4.2.1)**:
   Each fixpoint functor is checked at polarity `π ∈ {+, ◦}` under `π⁻¹ Δ` with recursion variable
   `(π, *)`: positive functors (`π = +`) are monotone and admit functor-changing subtyping
   (`mu_pos`/`nu_pos`), while mixed-variance functors (`π = ◦`) are interpreted by iterating from
   `∅` (`μ`) or `SN` (`ν`) and admit size-changing / functor-equivalence subtyping
   (`mu_eqv`/`nu_eqv`).
5. **Lexicographic termination (`LexBlockTy`, Theorem 6)** and **Algorithm 1–2 inference
   (`Encode`, `InferPat`, `InferSpine`, `Compare`, `InferTm`, `CheckTm`, `solve`,
   `Algorithm2Accepts`, Theorems 7–15, Lemmas 3–5)**.

## Where V5 Departs from the Thesis (Repairs and Design Decisions)

Each departure from the literal text of Nisht & Abel (2024) either repairs a soundness counterexample
identified in V1/V2 or chooses a capture-avoiding representation compatible with mechanized proof:

1. **Locally nameless binding for both type and size variables (repairs V2 capture counterexample)**:
   - Bound type variables (`Ty.var k`) and bound size variables (`Size.bvar k n`) use de Bruijn
     indices; free type variables (`Ty.fvar X`) and free size variables (`Size.var i n`) use names.
   - Syntactic substitutions (`Ty.substTy`, `Ty.openSz`, `Ty.substFTy`, `Ty.substSize`) only ever
     substitute locally closed types or sizes, and binder rules (`SubTy.lam`, `SubTy.allT`,
     `SubTy.allS`, `PatTy.cons`, `SpineTy.proj`, `Compare.lam`, `Compare.mu_pos`, etc.) open
     binders with fresh free names (`X ∉ Δ.dom`, `j ∉ Ψ.dom`). Variable capture is impossible by
     construction.
   - In inference, rigid size variables (`RigidName`, even `Nat`s) and flexible size variables
     (`FlexName`, odd `Nat`s) occupy disjoint namespaces, so flexible substitutions `φ` with
     `FlexDom φ` leave all rigid variables unchanged (`FlexDom.lookup_rigid`).

2. **Cardinality-based limit ordinal `Ω = (Order.succ (#Tm)).ord` for `∞` (repairs `∞ = ω`)**:
   - Higher-order inductive and coinductive functors (such as `Bush^a` or infinitely branching
     trees) do not close at `ω`. V5 interprets `∞ + n` as `Ω + n`, where `Ω` is the initial ordinal
     of the successor cardinal of `Tm`. Every monotone or antitone sequence of term sets stabilizes
     strictly below `Ω` (`muSet_ge_omega`, `nuSet_ge_omega` in `Solutions/V5/Stable.lean`).

3. **Bound normalization `a^↑` and `ValidVal` without global `< Ω` (repairs `∞ + n` size arithmetic)**:
   - In the thesis, `∞ + 1 = ∞` is treated as an identity while `i < ∞` is assumed for all size
     variables. In ordinal arithmetic with `∞ + n = Ω + n`, `i` bounded by `∞ + 1` can take value
     `Ω`. V5's `ValidVal W Ψ ρ` requires only `ρ i < ⟦a⟧_ρ` for each `(i, a) ∈ Ψ`; `SizeLt.var_inf`
     concludes `i + n < ∞ + m` only when the bound `a` of `i` satisfies `a < ∞ + 1`; and
     constructors, projections, and fixpoint subtyping compare sizes against normalized bounds
     `a^↑` (`(∞ + n)^↑ = ∞ + 1`), bridged at `≥ Ω` by stage stabilization (`Stable.lean`).
   - In `SubTy.mu_pos`/`mu_eqv`/`nu_pos`/`nu_eqv`, the size premise is `(SizeLe Ψ a.up b.up ∨
     SizeLe Ψ a b.up)`, which includes both declarative normalized subtyping (`a^↑ ≤ b^↑`) and the
     constraint form (`a ≤ b^↑`) emitted by `Compare`.

4. **Simple-kind-indexed semantic domains (`Sem : SKind → Type`, `PSem`)**:
   - Because subkinding `SubKind` changes only variance annotations `π` while preserving simple
     kinds `κ.erase : SKind`, V5 indexes the carrier `Sem ι` by `SKind` and interprets every type
     variable as a polymorphic family `PSem = (ι : SKind) → Sem ι`. Type substitution
     `interp_substTy` therefore requires no kinding derivation and no dependent type casts across
     subkinding steps, while `KRel W κ ι` enforces variance and candidacy at each kind `κ`.

5. **Positive vs. mixed-variance fixpoint subtyping (`mu_pos`/`mu_eqv`, `nu_pos`/`nu_eqv`)**:
   - Comparing `μ^a S ≤ μ^b S'` across *different* functors `S ≠ S'` requires monotonicity of at
     least one functor; V5 separates `mu_pos`/`nu_pos` (where the larger functor is positive,
     `Kinding Ψ Δ [(.pos, .star)] 0 T' .star`) from `mu_eqv`/`nu_eqv` (where `S` and `S'` are
     mutually subtype-equivalent in both directions and may have mixed variance `◦`), mirrored in
     `Compare.mu_pos`/`mu_eqv` and `Compare.nu_pos`/`nu_eqv`.

6. **Curry-style run-time terms, weak closed reduction, and deterministic first-match clauses**:
   - Run-time terms (`Tm`) carry no size or type annotations; therefore `Ξ` and `Ξannot` in
     Algorithm 2 share identical clause lists (`d.clauses = d'.clauses`) and differ only in their
     type signatures (`EncodedDecl`).
   - Reduction is weak (never reduces under `λ{D⃗}`) and clauses are selected deterministically by
     first-match with blocking (`selectClause`), ensuring that stuck clause-headed terms at neutral
     positions remain stuck under spine extension (`ClauseHead.lean`).

7. **Closed function signatures and explicit world scoping (`DeclsWf`, `W`)**:
   - Every function declaration in `Σ` and `Ξ` is required to have a well-formed size context and
     a closed type of kind `*` (`SizeCtxWf d.sizeParams ∧ KindingC d.sizeParams [] d.ty .star`),
     and semantic validity `SemValid W` is parameterized by a run-time world `W` containing
     `sig ++ Ξ` with distinct function names and closed clauses, preventing the V1 forward-reference
     and open-signature counterexamples.

8. **No bound trivialization in `InferTm`/`CheckTm`, and executable solver verification**:
   - Rather than mutating pattern-bound size contexts via the thesis's imperative bound
     trivialization (`Ψ|_rig`), V5 keeps symbolic bounds in `Ψ` and checks constraints in
     `c.ctx.substBounds φ`.
   - Algorithm 1 (`solve`) is implemented as a total fuel-bounded function over the SCCs of the
     constraint graph, and `ClauseAccepted` checks `checkScope` and `checkCoherent` via the sound
     executable decision procedures `sizeLtB` and `sizeLeB` (`InfCheck.lean`, `InfMain.lean`).
